Executive Summary

Accenture Tripoli Tech Hub delivered a strong Q2 FY2025, with total net revenues of $4.83M, representing a 12.4% year-over-year increase driven by accelerated demand in cloud transformation and digital advisory services. Operating income reached $1.02M (21.1% margin), ahead of the Q2 target of 18.5%. Headcount grew to 214 FTEs, with utilization rates at 87%, reflecting disciplined resource management. Cash position remains solid at $2.1M, with no outstanding long-term debt. The hub is on track to meet full-year FY2025 revenue guidance of $19.2M.
