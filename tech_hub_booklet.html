The unofficial-official HQ of the Tech Hub

Forget glass towers and sterile open-plans. Our headquarters is a place where roadmaps get sketched on napkins, sprint retros happen over plates of meze, and the best architectural decisions of the year are made somewhere between the second espresso and the first negroni.

Kallisto Lounge Bar & Bistro in the heart of Tripolis is where we gather, ship, celebrate launches, and occasionally pretend we're in a Sorkin-style walk-and-talk. It's not just where we work — it's where the Tech Hub became a team.
